**Definitions.** All figures are % a year (excesses in percentage points). **Gross** and **Net** are the model's geometric annualised returns before and after the entry and exit fees of every fund traded; **Fees = Gross − Net**. The return of the formation date *t* is earned in month *t+1*, and every benchmark is the reading of THAT month, with no lag (an after-the-fact comparison; the 2-month publication lag belongs to the model's decisions, not to this display). **CPI ES** is Spain's year-on-year CPI; **CPI ES+EA** is the plain average of Spain and the euro area. **M2** and **M3** are euro-area year-on-year growth. A period's benchmark is the arithmetic mean of its monthly readings. Years are the year the return was earned (2005 has 11 months, 2026 has 10). A regime cycle is a run of consecutive months under the same regime, with start and end as YYYYMM of the months the returns were earned; the regime that governs a month is the one classified at the previous month-end.

**Marks.** † M2 and M3 are not published yet for the last month (October 2026): their averages use the months that exist, nothing is filled. ‡ Fewer than 6 months: the annualised figures are only indicative.
