### Levers, adoption rule and survivorship (roadmap v5; owner decisions 2026-11-21; the values are final in `doc/decisiones/PREREG_P3_5A.md`, signed off and committed on 2026-11-21)
- **Six proposals, verdicts.** (1) peer-relative drawdown filter: KEEP, widened to the real-return filter (one mechanism: since-inception absolute filters lock in after a common shock; the funnel time series C2 decides whether it is relative only or relative plus a horizon). (2) anti-chasing: KEEP, one signal (trailing 36-month return z-score against the nature peer cell), Equilibrada only, subject to checkpoint D. (3) regime-modulated nature bonus and max_dd weight: KEEP, re-specified as a CONTINUOUS two-state trigger (stress = the eligible pool more than 3 % below its 12-month high; recovery = stress AND the pool's 3-month return > 0 at 2 consecutive month-ends AND the pool at least 5 % above its 12-month low; at most 4 complete transitions in the 2008 design window, at most 2 flips a year on average once R9 opens); never the regime label. (4) concentration caps: DEFER until D6 shows a material attribute effect (construction layer, R8; the currency budget exists; caps measured -0.11 pp, interval includes 0). (5) age-decay band: NOT ADOPTED (the drag was measured on R1, its shape is non-monotone, levers 1-2 attack its likely cause); the holding-age excess is reported at every band instead (Amendment 5). (6) survivorship haircut: an evaluation tool, not a lever.
- **Levers (at most 4, 2 values each):** L1 peer-relative since-inception drawdown and real-return filters with a lenient absolute backstop; L2 overextension penalty (Equilibrada); L3 two-state recovery modulation of the nature bonus and the max_dd weight; L4 one-off fees only: `return_ann_real_net = return_ann_real - (entry + exit) / H` deducted BEFORE the within-nature normalisation, H in {the baseline's realised mean holding period, 12 months}, w = 1 (management fees are already inside the NAV; a 2 % entry fee at H of about 3 months costs about 7.7 pp a year, so the scale is checked in C4 before the values are final). L1 reads the RAW `return_ann_real`, so L1 and L4 stay orthogonal. The static fee-aware CLASS choice is Track 1 (built OFF), not a lever.
- **Adoption rule (a variant becomes a forward-shadow CANDIDATE only if all hold; R7).** (1) Max drawdown <= 15.0 % for the live master portfolio as built (unmixed) on the full sample and in every informative R9 episode (window = episode + 12 months), unstressed AND under the primary survivorship tail stress (realised P10 path, a*k = 10 %, whole-portfolio trajectory, same for the baseline and every variant; P5 and a*k = 20 % reported). (2) On the track's KPI at the stated fees after the mean haircut (a = 10 %, k = 2): a Holm-adjusted lower bound over the full N above 0 AND a point estimate of at least 0.5 pp a year; if the minimum detectable effect exceeds 0.5 pp the historical test is non-inferiority (lower bound > -0.10 pp) and the forward shadow decides. KPI: L4 = net annual return of the live master portfolio; L1-L3 = the DD-constrained ceiling (`best_static_mix` on the variant's own costed sub-portfolio returns, one constant mix over the whole history chosen by the same rule for every variant, never re-optimised per episode, plus a line at the BASELINE's mix). (3) Non-crisis degradation <= 0.35 pp a year and the share of 36-month windows beating IPC+M3 does not fall. (4) The costed matched pool is beaten (R11). Reported, not gating: deflated Sharpe, PBO, the survivorship breakdown point. The two survivorship rates differ on purpose: a = 5 % (the locked base) in the tail stress, which kills funds SIMULTANEOUSLY in one window; a = 10 % (the conservative bound) in the mean haircut, which averages over the whole history.
- **Survivorship (FND-0436).** The panel has no deaths by construction (today's platform offer), so every PIT number is conditional on survival. The bias has opposite signs by lever: it UNDER-states L2 (the missing hot losers make the baseline look better) and OVER-states L1 (readmitted funds may have died). The evaluation reports the breakdown point (the a*k at which each verdict flips) and the tail stress; real attrition stays unmeasured until fund-level data on dead funds exists (DEFERRED ticket, review date set in the ticket).
