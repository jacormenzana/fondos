### 9.3 Ex-ante rules, fixed BEFORE any C1-C4 number was read (the freeze line in 9.4 carries the time)
**C1a, minimum detectable effect (MDE).** Series: the monthly paired net-return differences, variant minus baseline (band 0, monthly), of the grid bands {0.05, 0.10, 0.20} and cadences {3, 6, 12} months at 25 bp per side, plus the same bands at the stated fees fund by fund. These were already read as annual returns in the cost check; only their STANDARD ERROR is computed now (`pit_p35a_prep.paired_se_annual_pp`: stationary block bootstrap of the mean, mean block 6 months, 4,000 resamples, seed 12345, annualised as 12 x the monthly mean), never a new point estimate. MDE = (z(1 - alpha) + z(0.80)) x SE, one-sided, at alpha = 0.05 (reference), 0.05 / 11 (Holm over the 11 P3-5a trials) and 0.05 / N_full (the approved rule: Holm over the full N). The feasibility figure is the MEDIAN over the variants. If it exceeds 0.5 pp a year at the level the rule uses, adoption rule 2 is a non-inferiority test (lower bound > -0.10 pp) and the forward shadow decides.

**C1b, informative episodes.** Pool = the equal-weight pool of the unique funds eligible at each formation date, in any sub-portfolio. The pool drawdown of an episode is the peak-to-trough of the pool over the calendar months of its window (the return of a month is the pool's forward return at the previous month-end; the level before the first month is the first peak). An episode is INFORMATIVE if its window is complete and the pool drawdown is at least 4 % deep, and has recovery data if the data run 12 months past its last month. Only the drawdown and these flags are read: no score, no selection, no lever output (pinned by a test). If fewer than 3 episodes are informative with recovery data, the confirmation is descriptive and adoption relies on the forward shadow.

**C2, the form of L1.** For the 8 Junes 2014..2021 (the 2008-09 trough is out of every 60-month window and no locked episode falls in a June), in Equilibrada's Renta Variable: LOCK-IN GAP = (share passing the drawdown limit when the maximum drawdown is measured over the last 60 monthly points) - (share passing it since inception), both on the month-end NAV panel and the sub-portfolio's limit. If the mean gap is at least 10 percentage points, L1 is computed on the rolling 60-month drawdown; otherwise on the since-inception drawdown. In both cases L1 is peer-relative within the (date, nature) cell with the values "exclude the worst 35 %" / "exclude the worst 20 %" and a lenient absolute backstop. The share of filter failures whose trough is more than 60 months old is reported, not gating.

**C3, D6 and the scope of L2.** Outcome = the 12-month forward return (hindsight, 2008 design dates only). Equilibrada top gap: selected (equal weights) minus pool mean, over the top design dates; master bottom gap: master weights minus the equal-weight pool of unique eligible funds, over the bottom design dates; the pool = eligible with a positive final score; groups = `Fund_Nature` (today's value, a documented look-ahead). Shares are of the mean gap. (a) If (selection + interaction) / gap of the Equilibrada top gap is below 50 %, L2 is re-scoped to an allocation lever (nature mix) instead of a within-nature signal. (b) An attribute "explains more than alpha_persistence" if its share of the gap (attribute_mix / gap, within nature) exceeds 33 %, alpha_persistence's share of the Equilibrada score gap in D1; the two measures differ (a return gap against a score gap), so a flag only sends L2's signal back to the owner for review. (c) An attribute with a share of at least 25 % of either gap marks a P3-6 (concentration) candidate; below it the concentration caps stay deferred. Verifications run with it: the seal (9 hashes), the exact `score_base` reproduction on the rescored dates, and the test that the D6 loader leaves the score cache key untouched.

**C4, the values of H in L4.** Candidates in order: the baseline's mean holding spell (months, measured once on the sealed baseline weights), 12, 18, 24, 36 months. The annual penalty of a fund is (entry + exit) / H. For each H: the MEDIAN, over the (date, sub-portfolio, nature) cells of the design dates with at least 15 eligible funds, of the ratio (median penalty of the fee-charging funds) / (interquartile range of `return_ann_real` in the cell). H is a value of the lever only if that median ratio lies in [0.05, 1.0] (not cosmetic, not a veto). L4's two values are the two smallest H that pass. An unknown fee takes the median penalty of the cell and is counted. Entry and exit fees are today's values (a documented look-ahead). Reported, not gating: the share of funds whose within-cell percentile moves by at least 0.10, and the share of the selected funds with an entry fee.
