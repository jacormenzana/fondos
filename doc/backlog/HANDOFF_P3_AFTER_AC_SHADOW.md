## 3. Amendment 1 of `PREREG_P3_7.md` (section 13): the reading rules, in one place
- Series compared, all rebuilt from stored rows: **L** = the live arm (consecutive DRAFTs, `off`), **B** = the band-only chain (`P3_HYSTERESIS_SHADOW`), **AC** = this chain. One row per calendar month: the LAST `SHADOW` row of the month; gaps reported; the first row after a gap is excluded from chain statistics; more than 1 gap in 6 months postpones a review by a month. Turnover is RECOMPUTED by the reader between consecutive counted rows (never the stored `traded_notional`). Cost at the primary regime = entry fee on the notional bought plus exit fee on the notional sold, per fund, with the stated `fund_master` fees frozen in a hash-verified snapshot at each review. A row of month m earns the return of month m+1.
- **Before the first review only integrity fields are displayed** (status, scenario id, band, H, funds per sub-portfolio, weight sums, `calc_versions`, `n_candidates`, `n_fee_unknown`); turnover, cost and selection-difference fields stay unread until R6.
