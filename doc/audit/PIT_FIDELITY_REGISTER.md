# PIT fidelity register (P3-0 item 8), 2026-11-18

Every decision of the P3 roadmap rests on the point-in-time (PIT) backtest. This page lists the known look-ahead or approximation of that harness, the direction in which each biases the champion or the pool, and how the fact was established. It is read before any PIT result is used as evidence. It is based on run `out/reports/pit_backtest/20261117_201724` (manifest, `universe_by_date.csv`, `summary.txt`) and on the inventory; items marked **to verify** were not checked in code.

Direction codes: **F** favourable to the model (inflates apparent skill or level), **U** unfavourable, **N** neutral or conservative, **?** unknown.

## Consequences for the roadmap
- Items 1, 2 and 4 are **F**: absolute levels and nature-conditioned results are optimistic, and no arm of the construction study can remove that. Adoption rules (R7) therefore depend on the blind months and on a survivorship check, as planned.
- Item 5 and 6 are why the pool benchmark is rebuilt (P3-1.4) before any construction conclusion.
- Before the construction study, decide whether it uses the default universe (retired funds included) or the survivor-only one; the choice goes in the prereg (P3-2' item 3). The 2026-11-17 run is survivor-only, so P3-2' needs a new PIT run, or at least a cached rebuild.
